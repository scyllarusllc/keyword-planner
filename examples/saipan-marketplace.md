# Example: a local Saipan marketplace

This is an illustrative brainstorming example, not completed market research. No search volumes, rankings, or domain availability have been measured.

## Brief

A founder wants to connect people in Saipan through rental listings, supermarket promotions, and local supply and demand. Initial assumptions: a mobile-first experience and Chinese- and English-speaking audiences. Audience languages and product scope still need validation.

## Possible directions

| Direction | User task | What to validate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rentals first | Find a suitable place and contact the landlord | Listing freshness, owner participation, and renter needs |
| Promotions first | See which nearby stores have useful deals | Whether stores will supply timely promotions |
| Broad community board | Find or offer local goods and services | Which categories can attract enough recurring activity |

A reasonable first hypothesis is to pick one recurring task and build sufficient supply before expanding. These directions are not ranked by measured demand.

## Seed keyword hypotheses

| Candidate query | Language | Intent | Possible destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Saipan apartments for rent | English | Find rental options | Rental category |
| 塞班租房 | Chinese | Find rental options | Chinese rental category |
| Saipan grocery deals | English | Find current offers | Current supermarket promotions |
| 塞班二手买卖 | Chinese | Buy or sell used goods | Used goods category |

All four queries are generated hypotheses, not verified search demand. Different language versions should be useful for their audiences rather than automatic word-for-word pages.

## Next validation

Interview potential users and listing providers, inspect current search results in the intended market, and obtain compatible keyword data if available. Then choose a launch category and replace assumptions with observed evidence.
